All of this gear is part of the CiscoSzpont/[Firma X](https://www.firmax.kier.ovh/index.php) project.  
It is connected together most of the time, with the [Cisco 897VA](equipment/networking/cisco-897va) being the brains of the operation, the [Netgear WNDR3700 v1](equipment/networking/netgear-wndr3700-v1) and [MikroTik RB951G](equipment/networking/mikrotik-rb951g-2hnd) acting as Customer Premises Equipment, and the [ASUS WR-AX1800U](equipment/networking/asus-wr-ax1800u) acting as an access point with multiple SSID's for the VLAN's on the Cisco.
