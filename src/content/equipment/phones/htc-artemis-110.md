## HTC Artemis 110

*also known as the SPV M650, HTC P3300 or HTC ARTE110*
The first phone I got as a collection item. I got it from my dad in March, 2023 who said I could just take it because it's been in a drawer for ten years.

It also got me very much into mobile development and later would inspire me to take on Windows Phone, which has led me down to make the [Live Store](https://store.live.net.co) later on.

I immediately liked the idea of maining it, as it seemed like a neat little device, so I slid the Virgin Mobile SIM card into the phone, and it turned into a magnificent little communications rig.

It slowly decayed as it aged and the plastics disintegrated more and more.

### The failure

One day I had enough of the case, and turns out, I already had another one at home, so I asked my dad if I could take it, he of course said yes.

I then went ahead and tore down the phone, everything went smoothly until I put it all back together and... the screen died.

"Fuck my life, this phone had such a legacy" I said, but then I put it aside to wait and see what'll happen.

### The miracle

Wait and see I did. One day I was browsing Vinted and came across another phone just like mine, although it was from Orange. As I had money in my wallet from selling Lumias I decided today's the day and bought the phone.

A few days later (12th of September, 2026) it arrived and I immediately tore both phones down and swapped the mainboards...

...it WORKED!! I am so relieved writing this, knowing that such a legendary device is back from the dead. It's really stupid how it happened, but 50 PLN for another phone wasn't a bad price to pay for such a Hero (literally, that's the codename).
