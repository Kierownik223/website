## Nokia Lumia 930 Gold

Bought on Vinted for 80 PLN + shipping. Kinda beat up but I don't mind it.  
Works fine on Orange's LTE network. Got HERE offline maps on it.

### Fun facts

- The original text for Propanda's "Playgirl" was written with OneNote on this phone
- The proximity sensor likes to randomly stop working
- It lasts 2 days on battery
