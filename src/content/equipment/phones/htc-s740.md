## HTC S740 (ROSE100)

My first non-touchscreen Windows Mobile phone (so running Windows Mobile Standard edition instead of Professional).  
Found this model when I was looking at photos of the Touch Diamond online and wanted it ever since.  
Bought for 190 PLN on Vinted in very good condition, only flaw being it's not got my native language (Polish) available.  
My friends got a good play of Solitaire out of it, which is kind of the case with these Windows Mobile devices and my friendgroup, they always find a cool game for it and borrow the phones lol.

The day after I got it I went ahead and flashed HSPL on it, then found a WWE (WorldWide English) ROM for it and flashed it so that it had Polish (originally it was running the NLD \[Netherlands\] ROM).  
After that I decided to look on [XDA](https://xdaforums.com/f/s740-s743-rom-development.459/) and found myself [a ROM kitchen](https://xdaforums.com/t/vox-rose-kitchen-2-0-wm-6-1-wm-6-5-now-with-instructions.673237/) and thought it was a good idea to cook myself a minimal ROM (in the meantime I flashed the authors' WM6.5 ROM but found it boring asf).  
I managed to follow thru the instructions, get a little bit confused at the end using HTC ROM tool but managed to make an NBH file and flash it. I made my ROM exclude some BS and all the languages except English UK and Polish (fuck English US) and include Java, as I think it's neat way to run more apps on this device as nearly no native apps exist for Windows Mobile "Smartphones"

### Fun facts

- I bought this phone with the money I made from selling Lumias
- It's the most impractical phone I have ever owned
- The Wi-Fi setup is confusing, although many custom ROMs have remedied that (I managed to get it to connect to my OvisLink after some trial and error)
- The SIM tray is not located under the battery for some reason
